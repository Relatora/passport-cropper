Passport Photo Cropper

Upload a scan with several passport photos and get each one found, straightened and cropped to the exact print size, ready to download as JPEG files or a ZIP. Everything happens in your browser: your photos are never uploaded.

What it handles

  • Loose photos on a scanner, at any angle, even white-background photos on a white scanner bed.
  • An ordinary photo of one or more people: one passport crop per face, framed by face detection.
  • Printed photo sheets, such as a 4 × 6 in print with a grid of passport photos.

Photo sizes

35 × 45 mm (UK, EU, Schengen, India, Australia), 2 × 2 in (US passport and visa), 33 × 48 mm (China visa), 35 × 35 mm (Indonesia, Malaysia), 50 × 70 mm (Canada), or a custom size, exported at 300 or 600 dpi.
